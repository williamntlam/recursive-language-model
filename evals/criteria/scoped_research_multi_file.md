# Scoped multi-file research rubric

This opt-in repository evaluation rewards a source-grounded answer produced by
a bounded research workflow, not broad repeated scans.

| Criterion | Points | A strong answer |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Staged, bounded investigation | 0–3 | Starts from compact inventory/classification findings and limits semantic inspection to selected evidence spans. |
| Citation discipline | 0–3 | Uses a small representative set of valid `src/...:line` citations rather than an exhaustive or uncited path dump. |
| Technical synthesis | 0–3 | Compares the selected implementations accurately and distinguishes direct evidence from inference. |
| Efficient routing | 0–1 | Avoids repeated full-repository scans and uses scoped child work only for selected oversized spans. |

Penalize repeated global searches, per-file repository-wide grep loops, and
claims unsupported by the cited snippets. A passing answer needs at least 7/10,
at least one point for citation discipline, and at least one usable citation.
